# Sega - Dreamcast

[English](README.md) · [Español](README.es.md)

Sega Dreamcast artwork and metadata for [OpenCabinet-BDD](https://github.com/Utogu9/OpenCabinet-BDD).

## Sources and credits

- Original artwork: [libretro-thumbnails](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails/libretro-thumbnails) and its contributors.
- Additional back covers and inlays: [Sega Retro](https://segaretro.org/) and its contributors. Each downloaded image's source page and uploader are recorded in `catalog.json`.
- Additional Sonic covers, inlays and disc scans: [Sonic Retro](https://info.sonicretro.org/) and the uploaders credited in each image's `sources` records in `catalog.json`.
- Game names and serials: [Redump](https://redump.info/).
- Game and console metadata: [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) and its contributors, under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Console logo: Wikimedia Commons; its source, author and license are recorded in `console_artwork.json`.

Artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. This repository does not assign it a new license.
